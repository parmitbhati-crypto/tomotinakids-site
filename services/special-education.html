Who this may support

Every child learns differently.

Our Special Education Program supports children who benefit from personalised instruction, additional structure, or a different pace of learning.

  • Literacy, numeracy, comprehension, or writing needs
  • Attention, classroom participation, or learning-readiness needs
  • Dyslexia, learning disabilities, autism, or other developmental differences
  • Confidence and independence in everyday learning

Our process

A clear plan, reviewed together.

How we teach

Structured methods, thoughtfully selected.

Depending on assessment findings and the child’s goals, teaching may draw on Orton-Gillingham and Wilson Reading System principles alongside individualised instruction in literacy, numeracy, cognition, communication, and classroom skills.

Methods are selected by the team for the individual child; naming an approach does not imply a guaranteed result.

Skills we may work on
  • Reading and written expression
  • Comprehension and problem-solving
  • Attention and classroom readiness
  • Visual perception and cognition
  • Independent learning and confidence
