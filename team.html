Founder leadership

Nitin Bhati

Founder & Director · Special Educator

Nitin Bhati brings nearly a decade of focused experience in special education, supported by an MA in Education and a specialization in Special Education for Multiple Disabilities. His leadership has shaped Tomotina Kids into a centre rooted in early intervention, individualized support and inclusive care. He is deeply committed to recognizing every child’s unique potential and building programs that combine professional structure with warmth, dignity and family-centered guidance.

Experience, empathy and individualized care
