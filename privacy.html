Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Tomotina Kids Development Centre respects the privacy of families, visitors, applicants, and staff. This policy explains how information submitted through this website is handled.

Information we collect

  • Contact information such as name, phone number, and email address.
  • Enquiry information voluntarily shared about a child’s age, needs, or requested support.
  • Career application details sent by an applicant.
  • Basic campaign parameters, such as the source of a website visit, stored temporarily in the visitor’s browser.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, arrange visits, recommend relevant programs, manage applications, and improve communication. We do not sell personal information.

Children’s information

Please share only the minimum information needed for an initial enquiry. Do not submit medical records or highly sensitive documents through the public website. Detailed records should be shared only through a method agreed with the centre.

Storage and access

Website enquiries are stored in our secured service systems and accessed only by authorized team members who need the information to respond. We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected or as required by law.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted by contacting tomotinaofficial@gmail.com. Some records may need to be retained where legally required.
