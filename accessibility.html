Inclusive access

Accessibility Statement

Tomotina Kids is committed to making its website understandable and usable for as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies.

What we support

  • Keyboard navigation and visible focus indicators.
  • Semantic headings, meaningful link labels, and image descriptions.
  • Responsive layouts that work across screen sizes.
  • Reduced motion when requested by device settings.
  • Readable contrast, scalable text, and clearly labeled forms.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process. As pages and services change, we will continue reviewing content, navigation, forms, and media.
